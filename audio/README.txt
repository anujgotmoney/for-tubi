AUDIO FOLDER — songs play one by one in a loop:
  1. rehbara   2. sawaal

Drop your song as:  rehbara.mp3 / sawaal.mp3
(.mp3, .m4a, .ogg or .wav — site auto-detects the extension)

To change the order, edit the "tracks" list near the bottom of index.html.
To rename what shows on the music chip, edit name1/name2 in content.js.
